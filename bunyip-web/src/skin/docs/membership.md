# Membership & access

Some apps are open to anyone with an account; others need an active membership. This page explains how membership works and how to start one.

## Do I need a membership?

If you open a members-only app without an active membership, you are sent to the **Membership** page instead of the download. Apps that do not require membership are available as soon as you are signed in.

## Start a membership

1. Open the **Membership** page from the navigation.
2. Review the plan and choose to subscribe. Checkout is handled securely, so you will need a payment method.
3. Once your membership is active, return to the app and download it. The membership badge on your dashboard reflects your current status.

See the **Pricing** page for current plans.

## Manage or cancel

Open **Membership** or **Billing** to see your current status, update your payment details, or cancel. The Membership page shows when your access renews or, if you have cancelled, when it ends.
